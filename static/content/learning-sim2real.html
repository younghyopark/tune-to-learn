Gains & Sim-to-Real Transfer

Sim-to-real transfer is the most sensitive to gain selection. Stiff and overdamped gain regimes amplify the reality gap: small modelling errors in contact dynamics, friction, and latency get magnified into catastrophic failures.

Compliant and critically damped settings act as a natural low-pass filter, absorbing sim-to-real discrepancies and enabling significantly more reliable transfer. In our experiments, policies trained with compliant gains transferred successfully while identical policies with stiff gains failed consistently.
